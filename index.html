Who I Am

I am a free software activist, hacker, amateur radio enthusiast (TB3AAE) and father of Alef.

My first encounter with computers was with the Commodore 64 in 1989, and I started software programming with the BASIC programming language. I sold my first professional software in 1993. So far, I have developed production projects in programming languages such as C, C++, PHP, Visual Basic, Go, Rust, Python, Solidity, and Dart. I also developed web and mobile interfaces with frameworks such as Vue, Angular, and Flutter.

Making electronic circuits and mechatronics is my passion and hobby, with completed commercial products. I have been using GNU/Linux exclusively since 1996.

Community & Foundations

I am a active member of the Free Software Association (Özgür Yazılım Derneği - OYD) and hackerspace.ist, one of Turkey's first independent community hackerspaces. I also serve as an instructor at the Linux Users Association (LKD) Summer Software Camp.

Blockchain & EVM Chains
Co-founded Chainly (chainly.dev). Expert in Solidity (9/10) smart contracts, customizing core Erigon nodes, and building real-time AI anomaly detection engines for EVM chains.
Deep Learning & AI
Fine-tuned Gemma 4 models to auto-generate MongoDB aggregation pipelines and Flutter UI code. Fine-tuned NVIDIA NeMo for Turkish ASR speech recognition.
Open Source Security & Aegis
